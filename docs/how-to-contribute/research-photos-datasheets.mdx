# Research, photos & datasheets

If you have access to a FireWire audio interface that has not yet been modified, any information you can share about it can be very useful:

* Good-quality photos of its exterior.
* Good-quality photos of its interior, including PCBs, traces, connections, and components.
* Version of revision from the internal components or PCBs.
* A list of the IC models used internally.
* If you have access to a logic analyzer, it could be very useful to have signal logs from the buses between the interface's chips, such as codec initialization sequences over I2C or SPI, clock frequency measurements, etc.
* Maybe you spent some time finding a datasheet for a component that was difficult to obtain, and you want to take the opportunity to share it with the project.
