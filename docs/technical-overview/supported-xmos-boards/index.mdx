
# Supported XMOS boards

This section contains information about XMOS-based boards supported by the [fuegocable firmware](https://github.com/fuegocable/xmos-firmware). Currently, the firmware runs on the [DXIO MC (3W)](https://www.diyinhk.com/shop/audio-kits/175-2805-xmos-multichannel-high-quality-usb-tofrom-i2sdsd-spdif-pcb.html) board from DIYINHK, based on the XUF216, and support for the [DXIO208 768K](https://www.diyinhk.com/shop/audio-kits/183-768khz-32bit-xmos-xu208-dxd-dsd512-dsd-reclock-spdif-usb-to-i2s-out-pcb.html) is currently being implemented while working in FP10 and Focusrite Saffire audio interfaces.

Boards from other manufacturers can also be supported as long as they provide access to the necessary ports of the XMOS chip. Some options worth analyzing are the [MCHStreamer Lite](https://www.minidsp.com/products/usb-audio-interface/mchstreamer-lite) and low-cost options available on AliExpress, found by *"USB-I2S XMOS boards"*.

There are also published schematics for boards that could be useful to develop an open source solution. Some options are:

* [ddabidov/Headphone-DAC-AMP](https://github.com/ddabidov/Headphone-DAC-AMP)
* [slerpxcq/openuac2_hw](https://github.com/slerpxcq/openuac2_hw)
* [youheng7185/xmos_xu316_cs43131_usb_aud](https://github.com/youheng7185/xmos_xu316_cs43131_usb_aud)
* [ptc22336/XU316-1024-QF60B_EVM](https://oshwhub.com/ptc22336/XU316-1024-QF60B_EVM)
